Detailed Description

Mixer channels 0 to 23 that play sounds.

A channel keeps its volume, pan and pitch until they are changed, including across sounds and before the first sound plays. Commands that return AUDIO_OK without a reply from the driver (play, stop and the volume, pan and pitch setters) are validated by the library, sent, and executed by the driver in the order they were sent; a later call such as audio_channel_is_playing() sees their effect.
